# iSoul with Surbhi | Holistic Wellness Resources

I am Surbhi Taylia, a Life Architect Coach, entrepreneur and founder of iSoul.

For seventeen years, I have worked with people through private sessions, group work and international practice. My work is rooted in more than two decades of study across Vedic sciences, astrology and ancient mystic teachings.

I don't believe in fixing people, because I don't believe people were ever broken.

I believe the foundation is already there: your strength, your instincts, your knowing and your capacity to create a meaningful life.

What brings people to me is often not a lack of capability. It can be the absence of joy, clarity or connection with parts of themselves that became lost between who they were and who they became.

Through my work, I help people reconnect with those parts and create a life around what feels true to them.

## My Approach to Holistic Wellness

I see holistic wellness as a whole-person approach that considers the body, mind, energy, emotions and deeper sense of self.

My work sits at the intersection of mystic sciences and modern understanding. Depending on the person and what they are moving through, I may draw from:

* Astrology
* Human Design
* Energy and somatic healing
* Meditation
* Breathwork
* Psychic and soul reading
* Swayambhu Healing
* Direct counsel
* Self-awareness and personal reflection

I do not follow a fixed protocol.

I read the person in front of me and bring together what is actually needed.

Sometimes the answer is in the body. Sometimes it is a pattern the mind keeps recreating. Sometimes it is a deeper truth that becomes visible when everything else becomes quiet.

## Life Architecture

I use the idea of life architecture because I believe a meaningful life is not something that needs to be prescribed from the outside.

Together, we look at what is already within you, what may have become disconnected, and what you want to build from here.

My intention is not only success.

It is all-rounded happiness and the complete feeling of becoming your best self.
