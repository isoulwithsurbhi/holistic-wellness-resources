About iSoul with Surbhi

I am Surbhi Taylia, a Life Architect Coach, entrepreneur and founder of iSoul.

My work brings together holistic wellness, self-awareness, mindful living and personal growth. I work with people who want to reconnect with themselves, understand their inner patterns and create a life that feels aligned with who they are.

My approach draws from Vedic sciences, astrology, ancient mystic teachings, Human Design, energy work, somatic healing, meditation, breathwork, psychic and soul reading, and direct counsel.
